Lesson 17 – Money

Reading: Managing Money in Everyday Life

Money is an important part of everyday life, and there are many things you can do with it. However, knowing how to manage money wisely is an important skill.

First, you need to earn money or make money. Both expressions mean receiving money from your work. Another way to obtain money is to inherit money, which means receiving money from a family member after they die. If the person was very wealthy, you might inherit a fortune, meaning that you receive a large amount of money.

Once you have money, you can take it to the bank and make a deposit, which means putting money into your bank account. Later, you can make a withdrawal, which means taking money out of your account. Of course, people need to spend money to buy things, but it is important not to waste or squander money. These expressions describe spending money carelessly or on things that are not useful.

Sometimes, money is tight, which means that you do not have much money available. When money is tight, it is a good idea to make a budget. A budget is a plan for how much money you will spend on different things each month. However, making a budget is not enough—you also need to stick to the budget, which means continuing to follow your spending plan. Otherwise, you could be broke by the end of the month. If someone is broke, they have little or no money available.

A person who is financially savvy understands how to manage money intelligently. One important habit of financially savvy people is saving money. It is also a good idea to have an emergency fund containing at least three to six months of living expenses. Living expenses are the costs of everyday life, such as food, housing, transportation, and bills.

Another option is to invest money. For example, some people invest money in the stock market because they hope to make a profit. However, investing also involves risk. You could lose money if the economy goes downhill, meaning that the economic situation becomes worse.

Imagine that you want to buy something expensive, such as a car. If you have not saved up money or set aside money for the purchase, you might need to borrow money from a bank. This is also called taking out a loan. The disadvantage is that you will have to pay the money back over time, usually in installments. You will also normally have to pay interest, which is extra money charged by the bank for lending you the money.

When you travel to another country, you may need to change or exchange money so that you can use the local currency. It is safer to exchange money at official locations such as banks or travel agencies. Otherwise, you could be given counterfeit bills, which are fake banknotes that are not legally valid.

Finally, you can use your money to help other people. You can donate money to a good cause, such as protecting the environment, helping people in need, or supporting medical research. Non-profit organizations often try to raise money for their projects. When you donate money, you give it without expecting anything in return.

Good money management is not simply about earning a lot of money. It is also about knowing how to save, spend, invest, borrow, and donate money wisely.
